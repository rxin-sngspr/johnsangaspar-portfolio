App Development - Flutter
Built RainDrop - A Self-Made Water Tracker App
Personal Project | June 2026
The Situation

John wanted a free, offline-first water intake tracker with no ads. No existing app matched what he wanted - a beautiful Material Design 3 interface, dark/light mode, interactive progress ring, and a real Android home screen widget. So he built one himself using Flutter.

What I Did

Designed and developed RainDrop from scratch using Flutter and Dart. Built the entire app architecture: Hive for local storage, Riverpod for state management, fl_chart for monthly hydration charts, and Material Design 3 with ocean-blue theming. Implemented an animated progress ring for daily tracking, quick-add buttons (250ml/500ml/1L/custom), dark mode toggle, daily notification reminders, and a first-launch onboarding flow. The Android home screen widget updates live with current intake.

The Impact
  • A fully functional, ad-free, offline water tracker that runs on Android.
  • Widget updates without opening the app.
  • Notifications remind John to hydrate at 9 AM and 6 PM.
  • Clean Material 3 design with smooth animations and haptic feedback - on par with premium apps, built for free.
