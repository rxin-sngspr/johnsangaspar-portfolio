I work as an extension of your team, not a separate vendor. I integrate into your existing tools and workflows, take ownership of the operational layer, and report on progress without needing constant direction. Most of my clients check in once a day or less.

I am based in the Philippines (PHT, UTC+8). I work across US, Canadian, UK, and AU time zones. My typical schedule overlaps with your business hours, and I adjust as needed for urgent needs or key meetings.
